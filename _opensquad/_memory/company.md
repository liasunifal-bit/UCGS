# Profile: LIAS (Liga Acadêmica de Inteligência Artificial em Saúde)

## General Information
- **Company Name:** LIAS (Liga Acadêmica de Inteligência Artificial em Saúde)
- **Sector:** Medical Education, Healthcare Innovation, Digital Health
- **Location:** Brazil (Mainly Academic context)

## Mission
Promote learning, research, and dissemination of Artificial Intelligence applied to healthcare, preparing medical students and professionals for the digital transformation of medicine.

## Target Audience
- Medical students
- Health professionals
- Researchers in AI and Data Science
- Healthcare technology enthusiasts

## Products and Services
- Academic workshops and training sessions on AI in Medicine.
- Scientific outreach and educational content (especially for Instagram).
- Data science projects involving SUS and DATASUS data extraction and analysis.
- Networking and collaboration across the healthcare-AI ecosystem.

## Tone of Voice
- **Educativo**: Clear and informative explanations.
- **Inovador**: Focused on the future and cutting-edge tech.
- **Acadêmico**: Grounded in scientific evidence and data.
- **Inspirador**: Encouraging others to join the digital health movement.

## Core Values
- Knowledge democratization.
- Ethical use of AI in patient care.
- Technological innovation in medical education.
- Collaboration and interdisciplinary research.
